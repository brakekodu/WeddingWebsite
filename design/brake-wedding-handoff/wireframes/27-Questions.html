Brake Wedding · Deliverable 8

Open questions for the couple

Decisions the wireframes deliberately leave open. Orange lines show what's drawn now as a default, so nothing is locked in by accident.

RSVP logic
1
Two questions or one?Step 1 asks "celebrating with us?" per person, step 2 asks per event. For invitations with only Ceremony + Reception, should step 2 be skipped and "accept" mean both?Drawn: both steps always; skip is an easy change.
2
Ceremony without reception?Can someone attend one but not the other? If never, group them as one RSVP line to save taps.Drawn: separate lines.
3
Plus-onesWho gets one? Must they be named before the wedding? Do they pick a meal?Drawn: optional name, then treated as a full guest.
4
ChildrenAre kids invited? Listed by name? Kids meal option?Drawn: "child" guest type and a Kids meal in admin.
5
Who answers for the household?Anyone holding the link can answer and edit for everyone on it. OK, or should each adult confirm separately?Drawn: one person answers for all.
6
Showing who's invited to whatOn desktop, show "Not invited" for Sarah under brunch, or just hide her row? (Mobile hides it.)Drawn: desktop shows "Not invited".
7
After the RSVP deadlineLock answers (read-only + "contact us") or still allow edits and notify you?Drawn: read-only.
8
Email at reviewOptional, required, or not asked? Do you want confirmation and reminder emails?Drawn: optional.
9
Note to the couple / song requestsKeep, change, or remove?Drawn: one optional note on the dietary step.
Public site & privacy
10
Venue address on the public siteShow full addresses publicly, or only city + date until a guest opens their invitation?Drawn: public addresses for public events only.
11
NavigationOK to merge Wedding Party into Our Story and keep 6 nav links?
12
Site-wide password?Some couples put a password on the whole public site. Wanted?Drawn: open public site; private events protected by token.
Admin & printing
13
Who gets admin access?Just the two of you, or also a planner or parents? Do any need view-only access?
14
Print in batches?Allow printing the ready invitations while a few are still being fixed, or only all at once?Drawn: "Print the 85 ready" option.
15
How will invitations be printed?Home printer (PDF), a stationer (print-ready PDF), or a stationer merging your QR codes (CSV + PNGs)? This decides the export format.
16
Answers by phone or textConfirm you want to enter RSVPs for guests yourselves.Drawn: "Record RSVP" on a guest.
17
RemindersSend nudges to non-responders from the dashboard (email / text)? In scope for v1?Not drawn.
Wedding week & photos
18
When does wedding-week mode begin?7 days out? The day before the first event? Who posts announcements — and do they need to go out as texts?
19
Photo uploadsWhen do they open? Do you approve photos before others see them? Who can see the gallery: guests only, or the public?Drawn: opens after the ceremony, approved by you.
20
Day-of contactWho do guests contact on the day (coordinator, wedding-party member)?
Check against the architecture before hi-fi
What these wireframes assume about the data
A
Responses per guest × eventNever one RSVP per household. Every screen assumes this.
B
Event invitations per guestMixed households (brunch for John only) must be possible.
C
Guest-level "attending" flagStep 1 writes it and gates step 2. Stored, or derived from event responses?
D
Meal per guest per meal-eventSo the rehearsal dinner and the reception can both have meals. Dietary needs are stored per guest.
E
Tokens are permanent once printedCan be revoked (shows the error page); never regenerated silently.
F
Fallback code6 characters with no look-alike letters, unique, with rate-limited lookups. No name search anywhere.
G
Invite-only events filtered on the serverNot just hidden on screen: they're missing from public page data and API responses.
H
Portal state is computedFrom RSVP status + deadline + wedding-week dates set in Settings.
I
Invitation lifecycle + validation checksDraft → Generated → Validated → Test printed → Locked → Printed, with the checklist on the detail screen.
J
Audit trailWho changed an RSVP (guest or admin) and when — this feeds Activity and Guest history.
K
Partial savesAnswers stay on the device until Submit, or save step by step on the server? This decides whether "partly answered households" can be shown.
